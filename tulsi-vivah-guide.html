Tulsi Vivah Puja

The beautiful ceremonial marriage of the sacred Tulsi plant to Lord Vishnu.

Tulsi Vivah is the ceremonial marriage of the Tulsi plant (holy basil, representing Goddess Vrinda) to the Hindu god Vishnu or his avatar Krishna (often represented by the Shaligram stone). This festival marks the end of the monsoon and the auspicious beginning of the Hindu wedding season.

Step-by-Step Vidhi

1

Mandap Setup

Clean the courtyard or balcony where the Tulsi plant is kept. Place the Tulsi pot in the center. Erect a beautiful Mandap (canopy) around it by planting the four sugarcane stalks in the corners and decorating them with mango leaves and marigolds.

2

Bridal Adornment of Tulsi

Treat the Tulsi plant exactly like a bride. Paint the pot beautifully. Drape the red Chunri over the plant. Apply Haldi and Kumkum to the stem. Offer Shringaar items like bangles, bindi, and mehendi. Place Amla and Tamarind branches in the pot.

3

Groom Placement

Place a wooden stool beside the Tulsi pot. Place the Shaligram stone or the idol of Lord Krishna on it. Bathe the idol, dress Him in bright yellow clothes, and adorn Him with a beautiful flower garland and sandalwood paste.

4

The Vivah (Marriage Ritual)

The head of the family or a priest performs the Kanyadaan. Tie a Moli (sacred thread) linking the Tulsi plant to the Shaligram, symbolizing the unbreakable marriage bond. Offer Akshat while chanting the Mangalashtak (wedding mantras). Offer the Mangalsutra to Tulsi Mata.

5

Aarti & Celebration

Perform a grand Aarti of both Tulsi Mata and Lord Vishnu. Offer a lavish Bhog of sweets and seasonal fruits. Family members and neighbors gather, light sparklers, and sing traditional wedding songs (Banna-Banni) to celebrate the divine union.
