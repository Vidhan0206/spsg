Our Legacy Since 1995

Established in 1995, Shudh Puja Samagari Ghar has been the trusted heart of Amravati's spiritual community for over three decades.

For more than 30 years, we have served generations of families, ensuring that every prayer is supported by pure and authentic offerings. We are dedicated to preserving our ancient traditions while providing the highest quality religious essentials.

We offer a complete collection of Puja Samagri for every type of ritual—whether it is for daily worship or a special ceremony. We promise that every item you take home brings positive energy, sanctity, and trust to your Puja.
